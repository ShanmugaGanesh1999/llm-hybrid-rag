# Change Management Runbook
<!-- page 1 -->
Acme Change Management Runbook for production systems, maintained by the Platform Engineering team. Every change to production must have a change ticket in the ACME-CHG project before work starts. Standard changes are pre-approved, low-risk changes such as certificate renewals and dependency patch bumps; they need a ticket but no review board. Normal changes need peer review from one engineer on the owning team. Rollback plans are mandatory for every normal and critical change, and the rollback must be tested in staging first.
<!-- page 2 -->
Critical deployments. A critical deployment is any change to payments, authentication, or customer data stores. Critical deployments need CAB approval from two senior engineers, recorded on the change ticket before the deployment window opens. The Change Advisory Board (CAB) meets every Tuesday and Thursday. Freeze window. No production deployments are allowed during the annual freeze window from Dec 15 to Jan 5, except emergency security fixes approved by the CTO. Deployments must start before 3 pm local time so that the owning team is online to monitor the change.
<!-- page 3 -->
Troubleshooting deployment errors. The deploy pipeline reports failures with error codes. DEPLOY-4007 means the canary analysis failed and traffic was rolled back automatically. DEPLOY-4021 means the deployment is missing an approval ticket; link an approved ACME-CHG ticket to the release and retry. DEPLOY-4033 means the deployment was attempted inside the freeze window. DEPLOY-5002 means the artifact signature could not be verified; rebuild the artifact from a protected branch.
