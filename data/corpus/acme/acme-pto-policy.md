# PTO Policy
<!-- page 1 -->
Acme Corporation paid time off (PTO) policy, version 3, owned by People Operations. This policy applies to all full-time Acme employees in every office. Part-time employees receive a prorated allowance described in their offer letter. Accrual. Employees accrue 1.5 days of PTO per month of service, starting on their first day of employment. Accrual is calculated on the last business day of each month and appears in the HR portal within two business days. New hires may borrow up to 3 days of PTO against future accrual during their first six months, subject to manager discretion. Public holidays are published separately each December and do not count against the PTO balance. Sick leave is tracked separately from PTO and is covered by the Wellness Leave policy. Employees on unpaid leave of absence do not accrue PTO during the leave period.
<!-- page 2 -->
Carryover. Employees may carry over up to 5 days of unused PTO into the next calendar year. Any unused PTO above the 5 day carryover limit is forfeited on January 1 and is not paid out, except where local law requires a payout. Carried-over days must be used by March 31 of the following year. Requests. All PTO requests need manager approval at least 2 weeks ahead of the first day off. Requests for more than 10 consecutive days also need approval from the department head. Emergency absences can be recorded after the fact within 3 business days. Managers should respond to PTO requests within 5 business days and should balance coverage across the team. Questions about this policy go to people-ops@acme.example.
