# Expense Policy
<!-- page 1 -->
Globex travel and expense policy. Meals are reimbursed up to $75 per day while travelling for business, including tips. Alcohol is not reimbursable. Submit receipts in the expense tool within 30 days of the purchase.
<!-- page 2 -->
Lodging is reimbursed at the negotiated corporate rate. Economy class is required for flights under six hours. Expense reports above $2,000 need approval from a finance director.
