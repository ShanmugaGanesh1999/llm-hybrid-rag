# PTO Policy
<!-- page 1 -->
Globex paid time off (PTO) policy for all Globex employees. Employees accrue 2 days of PTO per month of service. Carryover. Employees may carry over up to 10 days of unused PTO into the next calendar year. Unused PTO above 10 days is forfeited on January 1. PTO requests need team lead approval at least one week ahead.
